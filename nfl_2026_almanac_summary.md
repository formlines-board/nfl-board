## Arizona Cardinals
**3-14 | DVOA -18.5% (27th) | Proj. 7.1 wins | Playoffs 19% | Proj. schedule 4.0% (1st — hardest in NFL)**

**Coaching:** HC Mike LaFleur (Yr 1 — first HC job, ex-Rams OC). OC Nathaniel Hackett (Yr 1). DC Nick Rallis (retained from Gannon staff; Yr 4 as DC). Almanac calls it "one of the NFL's most obscure, unimpressive staffs." LaFleur scheme = McVay/Shanahan spin, possibly 3-TE sets imported from LA.

**Why 2025 went wrong:** 179.8 adjusted games lost — 2nd-worst in the 25-year history of the metric (51.7 AGL in secondary alone, league high). Lost last 9 and 14 of last 15. Note for bettors: many losses were far more lopsided than final scores — repeated garbage-time rallies that pushed totals over / covered in-game spreads (author flags it looked "a bit suspicious"). Michael Wilson's 1,000-yard season was built on 4th-quarter garbage production (26-329-2 in the SF and LAR blowouts alone). Brissett threw 7 of 23 TDs in Q4 trailing by 10+.

**QB:** Kyler Murray released (to MIN). Room = Jacoby Brissett (not named starter; contract dispute), Gardner Minshew (signed March), and 3rd-rounder Carson Beck (24, post-elbow-surgery arm, 12 INTs in each of last two college seasons). Placeholder year — organisational plan is essentially "get a QB next year."

**Personnel changes:** Drafted RB Jeremiyah Love 3rd overall despite already signing Tyler Allgeier and restructuring James Conner (Trey Benson still there). OL: 30-something journeymen Isaac Seumalo (LG, 33 in Oct) and Elijah Wilkinson (RT — ATL made no effort to keep him) plus 2nd-round G Chase Bisontis starting immediately. Passed on OTs Mauigoa/Fano for Love. WRs: Harrison Jr. (never healthy + engaged in same season; playing for 5th-year option/extension), Wilson, Kendrick Bourne (31). Josh Sweat (40% of team sacks) skipped OTAs, reportedly unhappy Gannon fired, 1 sack/4 pressures in final five games, trade rumours. If Sweat goes, edge = Baron Browning + Zaven Collins (3.5 sacks combined in 2025). Akeem Davis-Gaither gone (IND); Cody Simon (overmatched 4th-rounder) likely starts at LB.

**Defense outlook:** Should improve via health — Sean Murphy-Bunting back from ACL, Walter Nolen (dominant in 6 games), Will Johnson (first-round talent, chronic injuries), Garrett Williams (Achilles Wk 16 — note slot metrics fluctuate wildly), Mack Wilson back. But lots of maxed-out vets; Budda Baker (30) declining in tackling/coverage. Almanac ceiling: "isn't likely to progress much past league average." 2025: most broken/missed tackles in NFL on defense; base defense usage doubled vs 2024.

**Tendencies:** Led NFL throwing to TEs (35%) three years running; dead last targeting WR3+ (9%). Ran only 27% from 12 personnel (league low) and 24% vs nickel (league low). 30th ranked ST: kicker battle Chad Ryland (57/72 inside 50) vs Joshua Karty; punter/holder Blake Gillikin's return could fix both kicker and punting.

**Bottom line (FTN):** "Mediocre roster using a terrible process." A notch above the worst teams, hardest schedule in the league, placeholder QB, OL propped up by journeymen. Better fantasy team than real team.

## Atlanta Falcons
**8-9 | DVOA -6.4% (19th) | Proj. 6.7 wins | Playoffs 20.2% | Proj. schedule -3.2% (26th)**

**Coaching:** HC Kevin Stefanski (Yr 1 — 45-56 in 6 yrs at CLE, 2x Coach of the Year). OC Tommy Rees (Yr 1 — Stefanski handed him play-calling midseason in CLE last year). DC Jeff Ulbrich (Yr 1). OL coach Bill Callahan (first hire; run-game builder — CLE ranked 3rd/4th/6th in rushing yards 2020-22). New ST coordinator Craig Aukerman (from MIA, 9th).

**Big picture:** FTN projects bottom-eight on offense, defense AND special teams — jarring for an 8-9 team with a 12th-ranked defense. The "waiting room" team: never bad enough to start over, never good enough to matter. No 2026 first-round pick (traded to move up for James Pearce Jr. in 2025; that pick became QB Ty Simpson for LAR).

**QB:** Michael Penix Jr. tore ACL in November (5th season-ending injury in 8 years). Numbers were better than the discourse: 7.0% passing DVOA, 334 DYAR in 9 games. Tua Tagovailoa signed; **FTN projection is built with Tua as starter.** Tua 2025: 67.7% comp, 20 TD, 15 INT, -3.4% DVOA. Offense barely changed with Penix out (-0.7% DVOA Wks 1-11, -5.6% Wks 12-18, 20th both spans) — skill players and structure carry it.

**Scheme fit worth noting:** Falcons led NFL in pistol for 4th straight year (33.2%; 7.3% DVOA). Used 12 personnel on 38% of plays and were +21.7% DVOA out of it vs -6.9% from 11. Stefanski builds from the run, and Atlanta's 12-personnel run rate (+9.8% over expectation) already exceeded his Browns'. Expect even more 12/13 personnel with Pitts (re-signed 3yr/$54M), Woerner and new addition Austin Hooper. One tendency change: ATL ranked last in empty formations two years running but Tua loves empty (MIA top-3 in empty 3 straight years; Stefanski's 2024 CLE led NFL in empty). Play action: 44% on 1st down, league-low 13% on 2nd. Run game very box-count sensitive (5.2 YPC/+6.0% DVOA vs ≤6 box; 3.5 YPC/-18.7% vs 7+).

**Personnel:** Bijan Robinson is the engine (1,478 rush yds, 79 rec/820 yds, 115 broken tackles — most among RBs, 3.14 YAC/contact — best in NFL). Brian Robinson Jr. replaces Allgeier as backup. WR room is thin behind Drake London: Jahan Dotson (-26.9% DVOA), Olamide Zaccheaus (-32.2%) as WR2s; 2nd-round Zachariah Branch (5'9", slot/space player, also primary returner). Every other WR on roster: 15 career catches combined. OL: Chris Lindstrom elite (12 pressures allowed); Jake Matthews 34; RT Kaleb McGary retired → Jawaan Taylor (7th in PBWR but 13 penalties in 12 starts). Line: 12th adjusted sack rate but 23rd ALY, 29th power success — run blocking poor, Bijan created his own yards.

**Defense regression flags:** 57 sacks (franchise record, 10.1% ASR, 2nd) after ranking 30th in 2024. FTN treats sacks/INTs/young pass-rush as volatile. Big veteran losses: Onyemata, Floyd, Ebiketie (48 pressures between the last two), Kaden Elliss (elite blitzer AND coverage LB), Dee Alford (excellent slot). **James Pearce Jr. (10.5 sacks) in a six-month pretrial intervention programme after December domestic arrest — availability murky.** Jalon Walker (5.5 sacks on ~half snaps) and DT Brandon Dorlus (8.5 sacks) are the young upside. Run D was dead last in ALY up the middle. Secondary: AJ Terrell top-20 DVOA, Xavier Watts (5 INT rookie), Jessie Bates solid; slot is a problem — Billy Bowman was last among nickels in yds/target (10.8). Rookie Avieon Terrell (48th pick) needs to play early.

**Special teams:** Full reset — Aukerman, punter Jake Bailey (7th gross punt value), Nick Folk (42 in Nov, 95%+ FG three straight years, 18/20 from 50+).

**Bottom line:** NFC South projected worst division again; ATL can "hang around" if defense regresses a little rather than a lot. Very high variance 2025 (beat LAR/BUF/TB, lost to NYJ, shut out by CAR, destroyed by MIA).

## Baltimore Ravens
**8-9 | DVOA 7.3% (13th) | Proj. 9.8 wins | Playoffs 64.1% | Proj. schedule -3.8% (28th — easy)**

**Coaching:** John Harbaugh fired after 18 seasons (now NYG). HC Jesse Minter (Yr 1 — first HC job; ex-LAC DC, zone-heavy 3-4, former Ravens position coach). OC Declan Doyle (Yr 1 — league's youngest OC at 30, **no prior play-calling experience**, came from Ben Johnson's CHI staff). DC Anthony Weaver (Yr 1 — was HC finalist; ex-MIA DC, ex-Ravens DL coach). Minter will call the defense; system continuity is the whole point of the hires.

**2025 collapse:** 1-5 through a brutal opening stretch (BUF/DET/KC/HOU/LAR + CLE). 2-7 vs playoff teams. Lost Week 18 win-and-in vs PIT on a missed 44-yd FG by rookie Tyler Loop (28/28 inside 50 before that). Now lost 3 of last 4 win-or-go-home games as favourites. Defense: 29th pressure rate, 31st sack rate, 31st get-off, 30 sacks (vs 54 in 2024); 398 points allowed, most since 2015; 3rd-most defensive AGL. Marlon Humphrey career-worst (61.8% comp allowed, 10.1 yds/target, $26.3M cap hit — potential cut). Roquan Smith 0 sacks, career-high missed tackles (but best coverage numbers of his BAL tenure).

**Lamar Jackson watch — the key variable:** Missed 4 games (hamstring, back), left others (ankle, knee). Career lows as full-time starter in rushing (5.2 YPA, 5.2 att/game, 47.8% success). Career-high 10.7% sack rate (3rd highest in NFL) and 23.6% pressure rate; high bad-throw rates. Faced 6+ rusher blitzes more than any QB (13%) and for the first time struggled vs them (5.1 yds/play, -11.5% DVOA). "Tired of Jackson" effort story from Baltimore Banner; locker-room messaging issues reported. Contract restructured but NOT extended — $84.3M cap hit 2027. Doyle is his 4th OC and brings much more under-center play (Jackson has been fine under center by DVOA but expect a transition). Minter wants him "more on schedule," less pure improv.

**Roster moves:** Maxx Crosby trade rescinded after failed physical → Trey Hendrickson (elite get-off, 3rd fastest behind Garrett/Bosa; injury-shortened 2025) + Calais Campbell (age 40). Nnamdi Madubuike had neck surgery in April, "trending in a great direction," expected back this season. Zion Young (2nd rd edge), Mike Green (7.2% pressure rate — 119th of 122 LBs — but Minter bullish). **Center is a genuine hole:** Tyler Linderbaum walked (LV); three options have 923 career snaps combined; DeCosta openly said he'll add via trade/FA. Vega Ioane (G, 1st rd — no sacks or holds allowed in 2 yrs at Penn State) and John Simpson (returning, 3 unnecessary-roughness pens in 2025) shore up guard. Ronnie Stanley 5th in PBWR. Punter Jordan Stout gone (NYG) → rookie Ryan Eckley. TE depth thin behind Andrews (block-first Durham Smythe) for a 12-personnel-based offense.

**Concentration risk:** Derrick Henry — 300+ carries and 75%+ of RB carries in back-to-back seasons (only Zeke, JT and Henry himself in the last decade); backups have 285 career carries combined. Zay Flowers: 55.9% of WR targets and 62.2% of WR yards (both top-4 of the last decade) — "borne out of sheer desperation." WR2 = Bateman/Devontez Walker; rookies Elijah Sarratt and Ja'Kobi Lane need one to hit.

**Tendencies:** #1 or #2 in running in power situations for 8 straight years. Pistol 5.4% with 24.8% DVOA. Far better vs man (7th) than zone (23rd). Minter's scheme: lots of dime (LAC 3rd), fewest men in box (LAC 32nd/31st last two years) — expect fewer box defenders. Fewest defensive penalties in NFL (2.0/game). Great with sim pressures (-40.4% DVOA). Big home/road split in 2025 (2nd/7th on road, 24th/25th at home) — FTN says curiosity, not predictive.

**Bottom line:** "Arguably the most poised to make an immediate comeback" of the non-playoff teams, with a 28th-ranked schedule and KC/BUF perceived as stepping back. But the FTN 2026 tagline is "what if neither Lamar nor the defense rebound as much as expected?" — Jackson's aging curve and the first-time OC/HC are the doubts.

## Buffalo Bills
**12-5 | DVOA 12.6% (8th) | Proj. 10.0 wins | Playoffs 69.8% | Proj. schedule 1.4% (11th)**

**Coaching:** Sean McDermott fired after 9 years (98-50, 8 playoff trips). HC Joe Brady (Yr 1 as HC; QB coach 2022, OC since mid-2023 — so Yr 5 with the team). Brady continues to call the offensive plays as HC. DC Jim Leonhard (Yr 1 — ex-Wisconsin DC 2017-22, Broncos pass-game coordinator last two years). New OL coach Pat Meyer replaces retired Aaron Kromer. GM Beane retained AND promoted to President of Football Ops.

**What changes:** Only McDermott left — FTN says the projection (4th in DVOA, 2nd in AFC wins) would be the same without any change. Defense shifts from McDermott's rush-four/two-deep to Leonhard's 3-4 base with heavy sim pressures and more single-high. (Note: the Bills were already 7th in sim-pressure rate at 11% and had the highest pressure rate in the league on them.) Watch this: BUF were 2nd in DVOA with MOFO coverage but **28th with MOFC** — Leonhard's college defenses ran a lot of single-high, so there's a scheme-vs-personnel tension.

**Offense profile (Brady):** Ball control. Led NFL in TOP (33:09); a play every 30.75 seconds — slowest pace of any team since the 2007 Steelers. Went from 1st (2024) to 23rd in 6-OL usage; now heavy via FB Reggie Gilliam + blocking TE Jackson Hawes (with Hawes on field, 63% of carries into stacked boxes but 14% went 10+ yds vs 6.7% without). James Cook led NFL in rushing. Allen: 30.8% DVOA from empty (4th straight above-average year); only SEA had a bigger play-action/non-PA gap.

**Receiver problem is unresolved:** Allen faced the highest man-coverage rate of any QB (42%) and the most MOFC (lots of Cover 1) because nobody feared the WRs — 17th vs man, 4th vs zone. Shakir (3.7 aDOT, lowest in NFL), Keon Coleman (7th-highest tight-window rate; owner Pegula publicly blamed his drafting on the coaching staff), only Tyrell Shavers had positive DVOA among WRs with 20+ targets. Big swing: traded a 2nd for DJ Moore (29, career-low targets/route in CHI, guaranteed through 2027 plus $15.5M of 2028). Kincaid efficient but missed 5 games, <35% of dropbacks.

**OL:** Same five started 71.4% of snaps together in 2025 (4th highest) — that ends; David Edwards gone, LG battle between Alec Anderson and Austin Corbett (four major knee/biceps injuries in four years). Dawkins/Brown top-10 in PBWR but middling in pressure rate (mobile QB effect). Torrence a force in run game, contract year.

**Defense:** 31st vs the run; opponents ran 6.8% more than expected (2nd behind MIN), 5th-highest early-down run rate faced. 29th in defensive AGL — Ed Oliver played 3 games, Milano/Bernard overlapped for 311 snaps. Adds: Bradley Chubb (released by MIA), Dee Alford (slot, replacing Taron Johnson), CJ Gardner-Johnson, 2nd-rd CB Davison Igbinosun, 2nd-rd edge TJ Parker (production dipped in 2025), Deone Walker to nose. Milano still unsigned; LB2 = Dorian Williams or 4th-rd rookie. Benford went from 31st to **1st in CB1-on-WR1 rate** and stayed top-20. Hairston (2025 1st rd) couldn't beat out Tre'Davious White. Bills opponents drew fewest penalties in NFL (101).

**ST:** Ray Davis led NFL in kick return value (30.4 avg, six 40+ returns). Tyler Bass back from hip/groin (missed all 2025). Punter battle Wishnowsky vs 7th-rounder Doman.

**Bottom line:** Still elite on paper, NE regression alone helps them. FTN's implicit read: the roster problem (WR) wasn't fixed and the man who built it got promoted; "more likely the Bills were bound to catch a break regardless of who was in charge."

**Diggs vs FTN (preview 17 Aug):** Aligned on the facts, but **Diggs is more bullish on the offense: he projects it as the best in the league** (FTN 4th in DVOA, 2nd in AFC wins). His case: the run game has improved every year since Allen became good (excluding scrambles); the drop to a below-average pass rate was probably a one-year blip caused by the WR collapse; even a down Allen year (40 sacks in 2025 vs 38 in 2023-24 combined) is ~90th-percentile dropback EPA; and the receivers were hurt more than remembered — Kincaid (oblique, hamstring, managed PCL all year), Shakir (August high ankle sprain, worse than 2024, offseason surgery), Palmer (hurt Week 6, ineffective after). Health plus DJ Moore could push them "to another level (1 seed!?)". He values Brady's promotion for continuity. On defense he's closer to FTN: 2024 couldn't cover, 2025 couldn't stop the run; Leonhard's DEN secondary work is real but a lot of man coverage is harder with Benford than Surtain; personnel solid except LB; Oliver and Benford are the two best players and both should rebound; Chubb (30, second year post-knee) could be a big boost but the camp buzz is about leadership rather than play, "which doesn't seem ideal." Note: five division titles, four 2-seeds, zero 1-seeds. Net: Diggs > FTN on Buffalo's offense, roughly level on the defense; a second source arguing the WR problem is partly a health mirage.

## Carolina Panthers
**8-9 (won NFC South, lost WC to LAR) | DVOA -13.4% (25th) | Proj. 7.7 wins | Playoffs 33.1% | Proj. schedule -2.6% (25th)**

**Coaching:** HC Dave Canales (Yr 3). OC Brad Idzik (Yr 3 as OC; **takes over play-calling from Canales in 2026** — noted in the DEN chapter). DC Ejiro Evero (Yr 4; Fangio disciple). GM Dan Morgan Yr 3. Regarded as stable/competent regime.

**Context:** 10th-worst team by DVOA ever to make the playoffs. FTN's history table of such over-performers: only the ones with a great QB posted positive DVOA the following year. Whole projection hinges on Bryce Young, and FTN doesn't believe.

**Young:** Real improvement in sack avoidance (ASR 7.5%→6.1%; 2 sacks on 17 pressures vs LAR in WC). Very low turnover-worthy throw rate. But: DYAR only -184→-83 (35th→31st) despite adding Tetairoa McMillan; explosive-pass rate barely moved; worse under pressure, worse out of pocket, no better on scrambles. "Avoiding bad plays by avoiding risk altogether." FTN wants a more aggressive Young even at cost of turnovers. Fifth-year option exercised (guaranteed 2027). Offensive splits: 11th DVOA on deep passes (16+ air yds), 27th on short. 3rd best at avoiding three-and-outs. Only DAL ran less in power situations.

**OL — the possible excuse:** 23rd PBWR, 28th RBWR (but 8th ALY — strong-right run split: 3rd running right, 20th left, Taylor Moton). Young had the 7th-fastest time-to-pressure of 38 QBs. 12 different starting combos; RG chaos (Hunt biceps, Zavala MCL/calf, Christensen Achilles). **Ikem Ekwonu ruptured patellar tendon in WC game** after a breakout pass-pro season — out until roughly midseason. 1st-rd LT Monroe Freeling may start Day 1, Rasheed Walker (1-yr, ex-GB) as bridge. New C Luke Fortner (pass-blocking specialist) vs 5th-rd Sam Hecht (better run blocker). Damien Lewis (LG) good.

**Defense adds:** Jaelan Phillips (outbid PHI; $30M AAV — FTN: overpaid but not severely; nearly 3 yrs from Achilles, 1.5 from partial ACL; Fangio-scheme fit). Devin Lloyd (All-Pro ILB from JAX; 10 QB hits + 5 INT — first since Lavonte David 2013) gets the green dot; Evero wants him as his Zack Baun. Nic Scourton (5 sacks as rookie) and Umanmielen at edge. Panthers have been dead last in pressure rate three straight years and ≤25th in ASR since 2021; lowest pressure rate when blitzing (29%), 29th DVOA when blitzing. Run D improved from historically bad 2024 (5.51 ALY) to still-24th. Wharton (3/$54M) was bad; 2nd-rd Lee Hunter could take a job. Secondary run back intact (~80% zone): Jaycee Horn only ~30th in coverage DVOA, 29th vs WR1s but 1st vs WR2s (Mike Jackson, cheap, will get paid). Weak vs slot and TEs — 26th or worse vs TEs in all three Evero seasons. Reverse PA split: 6th vs play action, 28th without (allowed 2.0 more yds/play without a fake).

**ST:** UDFA K Ryan Fitzgerald (four game-winners, good kickoff placement). Sam Martin strong on pinning punts. Return job open (Etienne/Jimmy Horn Jr.).

**Bottom line:** Good process, weak division = "will probably once again win more games than they deserve." FTN's worst-case is the likeliest: 7-8 wins with the QB question unanswered. Play them as a team whose defensive front should improve sharply but whose offense has a low ceiling.

## Chicago Bears
**11-6 | DVOA 2.5% (16th) | Proj. 9.0 wins | Playoffs 46.4% | Proj. schedule 0.4% (19th)**

**Coaching:** HC Ben Johnson (Yr 2, calls the offense). Johnson calls the plays; Declan Doyle left to be BAL's OC. DC Dennis Allen (Yr 2).

**The headline:** FTN projects the **offense 2nd in the NFL and the defense 32nd** — the gap is roughly double the next-largest (LAR) and one of the most lopsided projections the model has ever produced. Essay opens by predicting Caleb Williams throws for 4,000 yards (Bears are the only franchise never to have a 4k passer; he had 3,942 in 2025) because they'll be in shootouts every week. Strong "overs" profile.

**Why the defense regression alarms are all lit:** 22nd DVOA in 2025 but 30th in yards/play, highest explosive pass rate allowed (11.4%), most 20+ yard passing TDs allowed, most yards-before-contact allowed. Take out turnovers and only CIN and DAL were worse. They survived on high-leverage luck: 18.3% of defensive drives ended in a turnover (highest of the 2020s); 9-0 with 2+ takeaways, 3-7 without. Recovered 10 of 14 forced fumbles (71%); **32.4% of pass breakups became INTs — nobody else hit 25%** (norm ~20%). 26th on 1st/2nd down, 9th on 3rd/4th — the predictive part is the bad part. 12th in pressure rate on early downs, **dead last on 3rd/4th downs** (pressure rate fell 5.5% when the league average rises ~8%). 8th in Q1 defense, 27th thereafter.

**And then the roster turned over:** Gone from secondary: Brisker, Kevin Byard (covered TEs — CHI was 10th vs TEs), Nashon Wright (best vs WR1s), CJ Gardner-Johnson — 3,472 snaps. Also Tremaine Edmunds, Andrew Billings, Nick McCloud. They played man coverage more than all but 3 teams (up from 24th to 4th) and were **30th in DVOA vs man, 4th vs zone** — with fewer DBs now. New: Coby Bryant (S, 10th coverage DVOA but boom/bust), 1st-rd S Dillon Thieneman, Devin Bush (range; don't count on the pick-sixes), 4th-rd CB Malik Muhammad may start. Tyrique Stevenson back in the lineup after being a healthy scratch. Jaylon Johnson (-24.3% coverage DVOA in 7 games) and Kyler Gordon (3 games) returning healthy is the real upside. Pass rush: did nothing — counting on Dayo Odeyingbo (Achilles Wk 9; 19 pressures) and Shemar Turner (74 career snaps) back from major leg injuries next to Montez Sweat. 3rd-largest gap between top two pressure producers. 29th ALY allowed; Gervon Dexter 2nd-lowest run-stop rate among DTs; Grady Jarrett declining. T.J. Edwards (broken leg in playoffs) on pace for early return.

**Offense — why FTN loves it:** +22.6% offensive DVOA jump, one of the 25 largest ever without a QB change. Williams 0.0% passing DVOA Wks 1-9 → 8.9% after. Run game: -29.9% DVOA before Week 5 bye, **19.0% from Week 6 on (best in NFL), 1st in explosive run rate and success rate** — 3.1 yds before contact (most in NFL). Pre-snap motion on runs went 44% → 68% after bye (22.0% DVOA with motion vs 12.8% without; passing 20.9% → 32.7%). Swift and Monangai both top-7 in rushing DVOA. Williams: 9th in 20+ yard rate, 2nd-longest time to throw (only Shedeur Sanders longer), outside the pocket on a league-high 29.4% of dropbacks but far better inside (36.2% DVOA vs 5.8%). # Appendix B — Recurring statistical flags FTN treats as non-predictive (useful when Diggs and the book disagree)

- **Turnover luck:** fumble recovery rate (~50% expected; zero year-to-year correlation) and INT-per-pass-defensed (~20% expected). Big 2025 outliers: CHI (71% recoveries, 32.4% PD→INT), JAX (71% recoveries), NYJ (1.7% of drives ended in TO — record low), CLE (2/9 and 5/19), DET (4/11 and 6/22), WAS (1/11), CIN (9 return TDs allowed), DEN (14 takeaways vs 25-26 prior).
- **One-score records:** DEN 11-2, KC 1-9 (was 11-0 in 2024), IND collapsed.
- **Second-half defensive improvement doesn't carry over** (CIN, NO, JAX cited explicitly).
- **Performance under pressure isn't sticky** (Daniel Jones).
- **DVOA against play action doesn't carry over** (KC 31st, CAR reverse split).
- **Third-down and red-zone defense regress**; first/second-down defense is what's predictive (CHI, LAC, PIT Q4 collapse).
- **Special teams "hidden" value (opponent FG misses) is luck:** SF (highest since 2017 Rams), HOU; KC had the worst luck.
- **Schedule:** FTN's own caveat that projected strength can miss (CHI projected hardest → 26th actual in 2025). Biggest projected swings: NE (easiest this century → average), CIN (→ easiest), ARI (→ hardest), LAC/LAR (2nd/3rd hardest).
- **CB coverage stats are unstable year to year** (JAX Montaric Brown, ARI slot metrics).
